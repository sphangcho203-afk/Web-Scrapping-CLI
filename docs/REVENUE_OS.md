# Revenue OS

This layer turns Internet Hands into the data plane for a managed public-web change intelligence service. Nexus, Telegram Agent OS, and RelayMeter remain supporting systems rather than separate first-revenue products.

## First offer

- Free proof: up to 5 public URLs monitored for 7 days.
- Managed pilot: 20-50 URLs with daily checks, weekly evidence pack, and human QA.
- Recurring service: larger watch list, alert routing, exports, and optional branded reporting.

The offer is intentionally service-first. Do not build a broad SaaS dashboard until pilots demonstrate repeatable demand.

## Qualification

Use `internet_hands.revenue_ops.score_lead` with normalized values from 0 to 1.

Weights:

- recurring monitoring burden: 30
- agency/client leverage: 25
- observable competitive-research signal: 20
- reachable decision maker: 15
- budget proxy: 10

A score of 70 or higher is qualified for personalized outreach preparation.

## Economics

Use `calculate_unit_economics` to enforce an 80% target gross-margin floor before founder labor. Direct cost includes paid APIs, browser execution, compute, storage, and delivery costs attributable to the customer.

## Approval boundary

This code scores and measures. It must not automatically send outreach, spend money, sign agreements, share credentials, merge production changes, or perform destructive actions.

## Evidence standard

Every delivered change should preserve:

- source URL
- capture timestamp
- content hash
- extraction method
- before/after evidence
- uncertainty when significance is inferred

Respect site terms, access controls, privacy obligations, rate limits, and explicit opt-outs.

## Metrics

Track qualified leads, drafts, approved sends, human replies, positive replies, pilots, paid conversions, recurring revenue, direct cost, gross margin, monitoring success rate, meaningful changes, false positives, and delivery latency.
