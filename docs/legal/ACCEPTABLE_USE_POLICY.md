# Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy version:** 2026-09-20  
**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

The boundary between legitimate internet automation and activity Internet Hands will not facilitate through the hosted Service.

## 1. Core rule

Use Internet Hands only for lawful, authorized purposes. Your instruction to an agent does not expand your rights to access a system, account, dataset, or service.

## 2. Unauthorized access and security abuse

- Do not access or attempt to access accounts, systems, networks, or data without authorization.
- Do not perform credential stuffing, password spraying, token theft, session hijacking, phishing, impersonation, or social engineering.
- Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, access controls, rate limits, or technical restrictions where doing so is unauthorized.
- Do not deploy malware, ransomware, destructive payloads, cryptominers, botnets, or persistence mechanisms.
- Do not conduct denial-of-service activity or intentionally exhaust another party's infrastructure.

## 3. Privacy, surveillance, and sensitive data

- Do not collect, enrich, infer, or expose personal data at scale without a lawful and legitimate basis.
- Do not use the Service for stalking, doxxing, non-consensual surveillance, or locating a person for harassment or harm.
- Do not expose authentication secrets, private communications, financial credentials, or other confidential data obtained without authorization.
- Do not build or operate unlawful biometric, identity, or sensitive-trait profiling systems.

## 4. Spam and abusive outreach

- Do not send bulk unsolicited messages, deceptive promotions, phishing messages, or automated harassment.
- Do not evade unsubscribe, opt-out, block, or platform abuse controls.
- Do not use connected accounts to impersonate another person or organization.

## 5. Harmful or unlawful automation

- Do not use the Service to plan, facilitate, or conceal unlawful conduct.
- Do not automate transactions, account actions, or high-impact decisions in a way that unlawfully discriminates or removes legally required human review.
- Do not use the Service to exploit children or distribute illegal sexual-abuse material.
- Do not intentionally manipulate public systems, markets, civic processes, or emergency services through deceptive automated activity.

## 6. Intellectual property and content rights

Do not use Internet Hands to reproduce, distribute, or commercialize content in a way that knowingly infringes copyright, database rights, contractual restrictions, or other intellectual-property rights. Automated retrieval does not erase source ownership or licensing requirements.

## 7. Resource abuse

- Respect configured request, crawl, concurrency, depth, domain, and provider limits.
- Do not deliberately create runaway loops, abusive monitors, or workloads intended primarily to consume shared capacity.
- Do not resell, share, or multiplex credentials in a way that bypasses account or plan limits.

## 8. Good-faith security research

Security testing is allowed only on systems you own, systems for which you have explicit permission, or within a published vulnerability-disclosure program that authorizes the activity. Stay within the authorized scope, minimize data access, avoid persistence or disruption, and report findings responsibly.

## 9. Enforcement

We may rate-limit, block a target, revoke credentials, suspend an account, preserve relevant logs, or take other proportionate action when we reasonably believe the hosted Service is being abused. Serious or repeated violations may result in termination and, where legally required, cooperation with appropriate authorities.
