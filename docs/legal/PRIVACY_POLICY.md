# Privacy Policy

**Policy version:** 2026-09-20  
**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

What Internet Hands collects, why it is processed, who receives it, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to users.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed through the hosted Internet Hands Service. Internet Hands, we, us, and our refer to the operator of the Internet Hands Service.

If an organization uses Internet Hands to process personal data on its own behalf, that organization may be the party deciding why and how that customer content is processed. The Data Processing & Retention Notice explains that relationship in more detail.

## 2. Information we collect

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Account and identity | Email address, display name, avatar, GitHub account identifier, verification state, and account timestamps. |
| Authentication and security | Password hashes, hashed session identifiers, encrypted TOTP secrets, hashed recovery codes, and verification/login challenge records. |
| Service activity | API key identifiers and hashes, requested tools, request IDs, provider routing, status, latency, credit usage, monitor configuration, and execution metadata. |
| Customer content | Prompts, URLs, files, extracted data, browser or sandbox inputs and outputs, monitor targets, and other content you direct the Service to process. |

| Technical information | Basic request, device, browser, IP, and security information that may appear in hosting, application, or abuse-prevention logs. |

## 3. How we use information

- Create, verify, authenticate, and secure accounts.
- Route and execute instructions you submit.
- Meter requests, apply plan limits, maintain credit ledgers, and verify payments.
- Operate monitors, connected tools, browser or sandbox sessions, and requested integrations.
- Send verification, login, billing, recovery, and security messages.
- Detect abuse, investigate incidents, troubleshoot failures, and protect the Service.
- Understand aggregate reliability and product usage so the Service can be improved.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce agreements.

## 4. Reasons for processing

Depending on the context and applicable law, processing may be necessary to provide the Service you requested, protect legitimate security and operational interests, comply with legal obligations, or act on your consent. Where consent is legally required, it can be withdrawn for future processing, subject to processing that is still necessary for another lawful reason.

## 5. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information for money and we do not use customer content to build advertising profiles.

- Infrastructure and service providers that host, store, deliver email, process payments, secure, or support the Service.
- Connected services, search providers, APIs, MCP servers, browsers, and automation providers when you direct a request through them.
- Professional advisers, auditors, or authorities when reasonably necessary to protect rights, investigate abuse, meet legal obligations, or respond to valid legal process.
- A successor or acquirer in a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal protections.

## 6. Connected accounts and external tools

When you connect a third-party account or tool, Internet Hands processes the identifiers and authorization material needed to maintain that connection. Some integrations are operated through external providers. Their own privacy terms may apply to information they receive.

Disconnecting an integration stops future use through that connection but does not automatically delete records already required for security, billing, or audit purposes.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses strictly necessary authentication and security cookies for signed-in sessions, short-lived two-factor challenges, and authorization flows. The current first-party application does not use advertising cookies for behavioral profiling. See the Cookie & Local Storage Notice for details.

## 8. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, to operate and secure the Service, maintain required financial or audit records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

- Normal web sessions are configured to expire after up to 30 days unless revoked sooner.
- Two-factor login challenges expire after about 10 minutes.
- Verification and recovery challenges expire and are replaced or invalidated as part of the security flow.
- Usage, billing, security, and audit records may be kept longer where needed for account history, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
- Customer content may persist in captures, monitors, browser/sandbox workspaces, exports, or backups until deleted, expired, or no longer needed for the requested service.

## 9. Security

Internet Hands uses controls such as password hashing, hashed API keys, server-side secrets, encrypted TOTP secrets, hashed recovery codes, HTTP-only session cookies, scoped access, request ledgers, and protected payment verification. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to request access, a copy or summary of data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or review of certain processing. You may also revoke API keys, sessions, integrations, and two-factor credentials through available account controls.

Requests may be limited where we must retain information for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal compliance, or the rights of others. We may verify identity before fulfilling a request.

## 11. International processing

Internet Hands and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use contractual, technical, or organizational measures intended to protect data transferred across borders.

## 12. Minors

The Service is not intended for children under 13. Users below the age of majority should use the Service only with the consent and supervision required by applicable law. If we learn that personal information was collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

## 13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service or applicable requirements change. The version and last-updated date appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated where reasonably required.

## 14. Privacy contact

Submit privacy requests through the private support or legal contact method published in the Service or official repository. Do not place private account data or identity documents in a public GitHub issue.
